The checked-in derivatives are art-directed for the derico homepage: 2:1 wide
frames and 9:16 portrait frames, encoded as AVIF, WebP, and JPEG. The regular
rows and established trunks show a managed woodland and reinforce the site's
maintenance and longevity theme without relying on generic bamboo or sunbeam
imagery. No generative editing was applied.
